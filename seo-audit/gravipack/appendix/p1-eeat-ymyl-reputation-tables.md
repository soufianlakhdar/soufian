## Regulatory and policy frame relevant to the claims (for the fix plan)

| Framework | What it covers | Why it matters for Gravipack |
|---|---|---|
| EU MDR 2017/745 (medical devices); ANSM in France | A product whose manufacturer-intended purpose includes alleviating disease, injury or disability is a medical device needing CE marking under MDR. Intended purpose is read from labels, ads and website copy | 'Anti douleur' / 'anti mal de dos' wording may imply a medical purpose; CE-DM status is unknown |
| Code de la consommation L121-1 / L121-2 (pratiques commerciales trompeuses); DGCCRF | Misleading claims about product properties and results | Applies to any unsubstantiated health or reimbursement claim |
| Assurance Maladie LPP (Liste des produits et prestations) | Reimbursement requires LPP listing (code, tariff, usually a prescription) | 'Remboursé par la Sécurité sociale' is checkable against the LPP |
| LCEN art. 6-III | French sites must display mentions légales: publisher identity, SIREN/RCS, address, publication director, host | Core entity-transparency item for Trust |
| Code de la consommation L111-7-2 | Sites showing consumer reviews must say whether and how reviews are verified | Review program compliance |
| ARPP (self-regulation) | Advertising ethics; the Jury de Déontologie Publicitaire hears complaints | Health claims in ads |
| Google QRG (Sept 2025): YMYL health & safety | Highest E-E-A-T bar; 'Trust is most important' | Back pain, musculoskeletal disorders (TMS) and children's backs are YMYL-adjacent |
| Google Merchant Center: misrepresentation and healthcare policies | Unsubstantiated health claims can lead to product disapproval or account suspension | Free listings and Shopping visibility |
